The fine print

Cookie policy.

Short version: we don't use cookies. That's the whole policy, but the lawyers wanted a page.

No cookies here

This concept site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party trackers. There's nothing to consent to — which is why you didn't see a cookie banner.

What about…

  • Your browser may store cached files (images, fonts) to load pages faster — that's your browser doing its job, not us tracking you.
  • External links (Google Maps, Facebook) have their own cookie policies once you click through.

If this changes

If the live site ever needs cookies (for example, remembering a catering quote), this page will say exactly which ones and why — and a consent banner will appear. Until then: zero cookies.
